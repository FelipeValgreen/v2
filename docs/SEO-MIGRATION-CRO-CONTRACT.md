# RINON 2.0 — SEO Migration Zero-Loss + Organic Growth + CRO Contract

## Purpose

RINON 2.0 is not considered successful because the new interface is published. The migration is successful only if it protects accumulated organic equity, improves qualified organic visibility and converts that traffic into measurable commercial intent.

The governing formula is:

**organic visibility × commercial intent × conversion**

Every product, service, architecture, UX, content, tracking and release decision must be reviewed against all three factors.

## Search Console baseline

The current-site Search Console screenshot supplied for the migration establishes the starting benchmark, not the target:

- Total clicks: **125**
- Total impressions: **6.13k**
- Average CTR: **2%**
- Average position: **14.7**

The screenshot was supplied with an approximately three-month filter. These values are treated as the migration baseline; exact date reconstruction must come from exported Search Console data rather than inference from the graph image.

The average position of 14.7 indicates a material opportunity to move existing queries from roughly page two into Top 10 and Top 5. The 2% CTR also leaves room for better titles, descriptions, snippet eligibility and search-intent alignment.

## Zero-loss migration rules

1. No relevant URL may disappear without an explicit migration decision.
2. Every old URL must resolve to one of: `KEEP 200`, `301`, `MERGE`, `REBUILD`, `410` or `REVIEW`.
3. Do not use blanket redirects to Home or generic category pages.
4. Preserve the specific search intent, topic relevance and commercial meaning of a URL whenever it has organic value.
5. Preserve useful backlinks and internal-link equity through the closest equivalent destination.
6. Commercial legacy URLs with distinct intent remain durable when consolidation would destroy relevance.
7. High-risk, outdated or unsupported technical claims must not be copied merely to preserve text volume.
8. Canonicals must resolve to `https://rinon.cl` and the intended durable path.
9. Staging must remain `noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt` must block crawling until explicit cutover authorization.
10. Migration redirects, blog redirects, production tracking and public lead writes remain fail-closed before authorized cutover.

## Growth objectives

Post-migration organic growth must be evaluated by:

- number of queries/pages in Top 3, Top 10 and Top 20;
- non-branded clicks and impressions;
- CTR by query and landing page;
- average position by commercial cluster;
- qualified local visibility in Santiago, San Bernardo and Región Metropolitana;
- visibility for product, service, B2B/institutional and custom-fabrication intent;
- SEO-assisted leads, quote starts, quote completions, WhatsApp clicks and phone clicks;
- conversion rate by organic landing page.

## SGEO / GEO / AI-search requirements

Commercial content should be explicit, structured, factual and independently understandable. Prefer:

- clear definitions;
- what RINON can evaluate/fabricate;
- inputs required to quote;
- process and scope boundaries;
- comparison/selection criteria;
- applications and use context;
- concise FAQs;
- breadcrumbs and relevant structured data;
- verifiable company/location facts.

Do not fabricate certifications, load ratings, lead times, clients, testimonials, engineering scope, welding qualifications or operational capacities.

## CRO contract for every commercial landing

Every commercial landing must answer, above the fold or immediately after it:

1. **What is offered?**
2. **Who/what use case is it for?**
3. **What information is needed to evaluate it?**
4. **What is the next action?**

Minimum conversion requirements:

- one clear primary quote CTA inside `main`;
- one low-friction WhatsApp path inside `main` where the capability is currently available;
- CTA copy aligned with search intent rather than generic “Enviar” language;
- clear distinction between confirmed scope and items that need validation;
- usable mobile layout without horizontal overflow;
- no false urgency, fabricated social proof or unsupported technical claims;
- quote form uses progressive qualification rather than one excessively long first step;
- qualification may capture type, product/service, quantity, measures if relevant, location, use, files and contact data;
- WhatsApp remains available as an alternate conversion path.

### Intent-specific CRO

**Product seeker:** show configuration/use/quantity/destination and quote product.

**Custom project:** allow photo, plan, sketch, dimensions or description; explain that final scope is defined after review.

**Company/institution:** surface volume, destination, target timing and specification/logistics inputs.

## Pre-cutover SEO gate

Before any authorized production cutover, verify:

- migration URL matrix;
- status codes and redirects;
- canonical tags;
- robots/indexability;
- sitemap contents;
- titles, descriptions and H1s;
- structured data and breadcrumbs;
- internal links;
- image/alt quality;
- mobile rendering and Core Web Vitals risk;
- real 404 behavior;
- forms and commercial CTAs;
- analytics / GTM / GA / Search Console configuration;
- conversion events for quote starts, submissions, WhatsApp and phone interactions.

## Pre-cutover CRO gate

Review every commercial page for:

- clarity;
- relevance to the organic query intent;
- trust/evidence quality;
- friction;
- CTA hierarchy and contrast;
- navigation;
- mobile usability;
- speed;
- accessibility;
- form completion path;
- absence of misleading commercial claims.

## Post-migration monitoring

Compare the new site against the baseline and monitor:

- 404s and redirect anomalies;
- index coverage and sitemap processing;
- clicks, impressions, CTR and average position;
- keyword movement by commercial cluster;
- landing-page organic sessions;
- SEO leads;
- quote starts/completions;
- WhatsApp and phone conversions;
- conversion rate by landing page.

A visual redesign that damages organic discoverability fails this contract. An SEO change that brings traffic but weakens qualified conversion also fails this contract.
